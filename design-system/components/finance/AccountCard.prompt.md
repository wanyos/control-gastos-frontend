**AccountCard** — bank account / card summary; masked number, balance turns red when negative.

```jsx
<AccountCard name="BBVA Nómina" type="Cuenta corriente" last4="4471" balance={3204.18} color="var(--chart-3)" />
<AccountCard name="Visa Oro" type="Tarjeta de crédito" last4="0092" balance={-512.40} color="var(--chart-7)" />
```
